About

Cultural Community in State College, PA

Aabahon is a non-profit organization established in State College Pennsylvania in 2022, dedicated to promoting India’s rich cultural heritage and traditions for future generations, and to give people the opportunty to experience and celebrate a home away from home during their time at State College.

The goal of Aabahon is to promote cross-cultural interactions via informal gatherings where everyone is welcome. Aabahon also serves as a platform to engage with students and community members alike, in its endeavor to promote vibrancy through events such as Durga Pujo, Saraswati Pujo, Nobo Borsho (Bengali New Year), Rabindra Jayanti, Holi, Mahalaya, fresher’s welcome, and many other such events. Come and be a part of Aabahon and our events, create new memories and have a good time!

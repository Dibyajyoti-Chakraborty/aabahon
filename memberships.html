Memberships

Membership Details

Embark on a journey to celebrate the rich Bengali language and Indian cultural heritage by becoming a valued member of Aabahon. We welcome individuals aged 18 and above, who share a passion for our vibrant traditions and want to be part of a thriving community. Please choose from our membership tiers below and join us in preserving and promoting our cultural legacy. Membership is annual - 1st August (current year) to 31st July (following year).

Membership Privileges

Immersive Experience: As a member in good standing, you will receive exclusive access to our membership emails, notifications, and publications. Stay connected and be the first to know about our upcoming events, workshops, and exciting initiatives.
Voting Rights: Your voice matters! Exercise your right to shape the future of Aabahon by participating in the decision-making process through voting privileges. Be part of the collective journey as we navigate the path of preserving and promoting our cultural heritage.
Incentives and Discounts: Embrace the advantages of membership! Regular student and postdoctoral members can enjoy special incentives and discounts on admission charges for our non-profit Aabahon-organized activities. Experience our cultural offerings while benefiting from cost-saving opportunities.
Community Engagement: Engage in a vibrant community that shares your love for Bengali language and Indian cultural heritage. Connect with fellow members, exchange ideas, and build lifelong friendships. Attend our general organizational activities and become an integral part of our close-knit family.

Join Aabahon today and unlock a world of cultural exploration, personal growth, and meaningful connections. Let us come together to celebrate and preserve the essence of our heritage!

Family
$100

A family membership shall include both legal partners, dependent children (unmarried, and under 25), and dependent relatives (residing in the USA for more than 180 days in a calendar year). This definition of “dependent children” and “dependent relatives” is considered for the rest of the document.

Working Professionals
$60

Open to individuals aged 18 and above, our individual membership offers you a gateway to dive into a vibrant community where you can meet like-minded enthusiasts, participate in engaging events, and embark on a journey of self-discovery.

Post-Doctoral
$30

This is for individuals or families where the highest-earning member of the household is a postdoctoral scholar/fellow. For families, the Postdoctoral membership dues amount needs to be paid by each legal partner separately. No membership dues are required for dependent children and dependent relatives, while they can still be members.

Student
$15

This is for individuals or families where the highest earning member of the household is a full-time student (undergrad, grad – masters/PhD). For families, the Regular student membership dues amount needs to be paid by each legal partner separately. No membership dues are required for dependent children and dependent relatives, while they can still be members.

Complimentary Student Membership
Free

Our complimentary student membership is tailored just for you. Enjoy the benefits of being part of our community without the financial commitment. Immerse yourself in the richness of our cultural events and forge connections that will last a lifetime.
